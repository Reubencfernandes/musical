# Instrument samples

FluidR3 GM by Frank Wen. License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 US.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Pre-rendered MP3 samples from Paul Rosen's fork of gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts:
https://github.com/paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulrosen/midi-js-soundfonts/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM/acoustic_grand_piano-mp3.js

The base64 samples were unpacked into individual MP3 files without changing the
audio, for same-origin ABCJS score playback. Bundled instruments: piano,
nylon acoustic guitar, clean electric guitar, acoustic bass, violin, cello,
string ensemble, trumpet, alto saxophone, clarinet, and flute.
