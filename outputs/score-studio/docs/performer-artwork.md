# Performer artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Each PNG is a four-by-four sheet of 16 illustrated poses. The browser samples these into stationary dots and interpolates dot area at display refresh rate. Original generated files are retained outside the project; the application uses the copies in public/performers.

## guitar

Asset: [guitar.png](../public/performers/guitar.png)

Final prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production animation sprite sheet to be sampled into fixed-grid halftone dots by an app. Create a square 2048x2048 sprite sheet with EXACTLY 16 equal cells in an invisible 4-column by 4-row grid. Each cell is 512x512, no gutters, no borders, no labels or text. Pure solid BLACK background, pure WHITE filled silhouette artwork with deliberate black negative spaces. Flat monochrome, crisp clean edges, no dots, no texture, no gray, no glow, no shadows. Each cell shows the SAME complete adult musician and the SAME instrument, identical camera, scale, clothing, proportions, and fixed ground baseline at 88% cell height. Reading left-to-right top-to-bottom the sixteen cells form consecutive small evenly spaced steps of a seamless natural playing cycle. Meaningful controlled pose progression, no duplicate static cells: slow relaxed torso sway and purposeful arm/hand motion. First and last pose nearly meet for looping. Keep head, feet, and entire instrument comfortably inside EVERY cell with at least 9% margin. Detailed enough to read as an elegant adult performer, simple enough for a halftone silhouette. Match a restrained jazz-club silhouette aesthetic. Only the musician and their own instrument/supports.
Subject and motion: A standing guitarist playing an acoustic guitar held across the torso at a slight diagonal, three-quarter side view facing right. Neck extends to the right, left fretting hand on neck, right strumming hand over round soundhole; visible negative-space soundhole and gap between arms and torso. Casual shirt and trousers. Strumming forearm completes a gentle down-up strum through the sixteen sequential poses while torso shifts weight slightly; guitar body and feet stable. The silhouette must unmistakably show a person playing a guitar.
